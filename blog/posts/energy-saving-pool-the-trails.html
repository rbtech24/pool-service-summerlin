What to do this week about pool energy savings

  1. Step 1. Prefer longer low-speed runs over short high-speed blasts when equipment allows.
  2. Step 2. Clean filters so the pump is not fighting restriction.
  3. Step 3. Do not slash runtime during dust season just to save a few dollars.
  4. Step 4. Fix leaks and bad seals—they waste energy and water.
  5. Step 5. Ask for a runtime plan that matches summer vs winter.
