Is waterline film the same as scale?
No. Film is often oils; scale is minerals. They can appear together.
Will more chlorine remove the ring?
Chlorine helps water quality but brushing and filtration matter for the ring.
Does this mean my pool is dirty?
It means the waterline needs mechanical cleaning and balanced care—not panic.
