Quick answer for Summerlin homeowners

Test the water fully, clean the filter if pressure is high, and keep weekly service consistent. That is the practical core of cloudy pool water in a desert climate—not a random pile of store chemicals.

What to do this week about cloudy pool water

  1. Step 1. Look at the water in full sun—can you see the main drain?
  2. Step 2. Empty skimmer and pump baskets so the system can circulate.
  3. Step 3. Note filter pressure; a jump after a windy day often means a dirty filter.
  4. Step 4. Do not stack random clarifiers on top of unbalanced water.
  5. Step 5. Resume a steady weekly clean so chemistry does not free-fall mid-week.

How professional Summerlin pool service helps

FAQs about cloudy pool water in Summerlin

Why is my pool cloudy after a hot weekend?
Heavy swimming, sunscreen, and high temps use up free chlorine. Without enough residual, the water turns dull quickly.
Will a pool clarifier fix cloudy water alone?
Sometimes it helps cosmetics, but it will not replace testing, filtration, and chlorine control.
How fast can Summerlin heat cloud a pool?
In peak summer, a pool can look fine Monday and dull by Thursday if the filter is loaded or chlorine drops.
