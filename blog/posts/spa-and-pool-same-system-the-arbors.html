What to do this week about spa pool service

  1. Step 1. Test the spa after heavy use—do not assume pool numbers match.
  2. Step 2. Run spa jets long enough after adding product.
  3. Step 3. Watch for cloudy spa water even when the pool is clear.
  4. Step 4. Keep filters clean; spa debris loads them quickly.
  5. Step 5. Tell your tech you use the spa often so dosing matches reality.

Weekly is smart if you use it. Occasional use still needs chemistry checks.
